PART B

The Do's (Best Practices)

1.)Do review your privacy settings regularly to control who can see your personal information and posts.

2.)Do curate a professional presence on platforms like LinkedIn to showcase your academic projects, technical skills, and portfolio to future employers.

3.)Do think critically before you post or share, remembering that digital footprints are permanent and easily searchable.

4.)Do verify information before sharing it to avoid spreading rumors, fake news, or unverified campus gossip.

5.)Do take regular screen breaks to prioritize your mental health, focus on your coursework, and build offline relationships.

The Don'ts

1.)Don't share sensitive personal data like your student registration number, passwords, or exact real-time location.

2.)Don't post unprofessional or inappropriate content that could jeopardize future internships or violate the university's code of conduct.

3.)Don't engage in cyberbullying, online harassment, or heated public arguments that can damage your reputation.

4.)Don't let endless scrolling distract you from your assignment deadlines, project work, or exam preparation.

5.)Don't use social media as a metric for self-worth or constantly compare your behind-the-scenes life to everyone else's highlight reel.
