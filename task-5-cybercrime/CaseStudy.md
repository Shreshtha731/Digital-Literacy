UPI / Online Payment Fraud

Case Study: The "QR Code" Marketplace Scam

What it is:
UPI (Unified Payments Interface) fraud involves scammers manipulating victims into unknowingly authorizing transactions,
effectively stealing money directly from their linked bank accounts under false pretenses.


How it happens (Step-by-Step):

1.)The Hook: A university student lists a used engineering textbook for sale on a popular peer-to-peer online marketplace.

2.)The Contact: A scammer poses as an eager buyer. They agree to the asking price immediately without negotiation, claiming they need the book urgently for an upcoming exam.

3.)The Trap: The buyer states they cannot pay in cash and insists on paying in advance via UPI. They send a custom QR code to the student's messaging app.

4.)The Execution: The scammer calls the student, instructing them to scan the code and enter your UPI PIN to receive the payment.
   Distracted by the prospect of a quick sale, the student scans the code and enters their secret PIN.

5.)The Theft: Because a UPI PIN is exclusively used to deduct money, entering it authorizes a transfer to the scammer. The funds are instantly drained.

Typical Targets:
College students, young professionals, and the elderly are frequent targets due to their high amount of online peer to peer transactions and occasional lack of alertness during quick digital sales.

Consequences:

The primary consequence is immediate, unrecoverable financial loss, as the victim technically authorized the transactions themselves. Secondary consequences include severe emotional and mental disturbance
and a compromised mindset for financial security.
