The Stay Safe Online Checklist for College Students

Financial & UPI Safety

1.) The Golden UPI Rule: Remember that your UPI PIN is only required to send money or check your balance. 
    Never enter your PIN to receive funds, especially when dealing with strangers on platforms like OLX or Facebook Marketplace.

2.) Verify Before You Pay: Before scanning a QR code at the campus canteen or sending money to a batchmate, 
    always double-check the registered UPI Name/ID (VPA) that appears on your screen to ensure it's the correct recipient.

Everyday Digital Hygiene:

1.) Beware of the Paid Internship Trap: If a recruiter or company asks you to pay a registration fee, laptop security deposit, or training fee for a summer internship or job, it is a scam. 
Legitimate companies pay you, not the other way around.

2.) Don't Trust Public Wi-Fi: Avoid making online payments, checking bank balances, or logging into university portals using open, free Wi-Fi networks in cafés, libraries, or airports. Use your mobile data instead.

3.) Audit Your App Permissions: That free PDF scanner or random mobile game doesn't need access to your microphone, camera, or contacts. Regularly check your phone's settings and revoke unnecessary permissions.

4.) Lock Down Your Devices: Never leave your laptop or phone unlocked in the hostel room, library, or lab, even for a minute. Set your screens to auto-lock after 30 to 60 seconds of inactivity.

5.) Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Turn on 2FA for your primary email, WhatsApp, and social media accounts. 
    This ensures that even if someone guesses your password, they can't log in without the code sent to your phone.

6.) Keep Your Socials Private: Audit your Instagram, Facebook, and Snapchat privacy settings. 
    Don't accept follow requests from strangers, and avoid posting real-time updates about your exact location (like tagging the specific café you are currently sitting in).
